# A link for “Transfer Walsh”

Research checked 9 October 2026.

## Recommendation

Use [Wikipedia’s “Transfer Walsh”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_Walsh)
for the introductory phrase in `src/README.md`. The link should explain the
convention generally; matching Muscat’s particular implementation is secondary.

Its opening paragraph immediately explains the two major-suit transfers, the
meaning of “Walsh,” and the purpose of placing more contracts in opener’s hand.
The rest surveys variations and advantages, with links to related concepts.
This makes it a suitable general reference for a reader encountering the term.
[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_Walsh).

Its first response table presents a diamond-showing 1♠, while later paragraphs
discuss other treatments. Readers should use Muscat’s own documentation for its
specific agreements. [Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_Walsh).

## Alternatives checked

- [Contract Bridge Fandom](https://bridge.fandom.com/wiki/Transfer_Walsh)
  gives an even shorter explanation and explicitly separates the two major
  transfers from variable 1♠-and-higher responses. Its indexed text was readable,
  but a direct web-tool fetch failed with HTTP 402 during this check.
- [Ray Green’s article](https://www.bridgewebs.com/brunton/page41.html#1454510882)
  explains the basics, then develops a particular system at length. It is useful
  additional reading, but its implementation detail makes it a poorer fit for
  this general introductory hyperlink.
- [Gerben’s introductory page](https://www.geocities.ws/gerben42/twalsh.html)
  is concise but centers a particular set of agreements.
- [Dan Israeli’s Bridge Winners page](https://bridgewinners.com/article/view/transfer-walsh/)
  is a poll about transfer completion, not an introduction.

This is an editorial recommendation for the README’s audience, not an exhaustive
ranking of every page online.
