# Transfer Walsh 1♣

Responses to 1♣ are Transfer Walsh.  Responder bypasses diamonds with a 4-card
major unless game-forcing, and shows the major by transfer.  With both majors,
transfer to the longer one, or to spades with 5+♠ and 5+♥.

The 1♠ response transfers to 1NT and denies a 4-card major.  Responder is
either weak, or long in diamonds, or both, or game-forcing in diamonds.  The
real 1NT then becomes a balanced invitation, and 2NT a balanced game force.

Inverted 2♣ is a limit raise or better.  2♦ is a Multi with a weak 6-card
major, and opener bids 2♥ to pass or correct.  This frees 2♥♠ for invitational
6-card majors, as in the Brink--Drijver card.

|-----|-|
| 1♦! | TRF, 6+, 4+♥
| 1♥! | TRF, 6+, 4+♠
| 1♠! | TRF to 1NT, 0--3♠, 0--3♥, (0--10 or (FG, 5+♦))
| 1NT | BAL INV, 11--12, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 2♣  | LIM+, 5+♣, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 2♦! | MULTI, 3--6, 6+♥ or 6+♠
| 2M! | INV, 6+M
| 2NT | BAL FG, 13--15, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 3♣  | PRE, 0--6, 5+♣
| 3♦! | PRE, 3--6, 6+♦
| 3M! | PRE, 3--6, 7+#
| 3NT | BAL, 16--17, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 4M  | PRE, 8+#
