# Transfer Walsh 1♣

[Walsh] is familiar territory in modern bidding: with less than game-forcing
strength, we bypass diamonds to show a 4-card major.  That leaves the cheapest
response doing little work.  As [Richard Granville][ric] puts it, "the 1♦ response
to 1♣ is severely underused."

So why not rotate the responses?  [Transfer Walsh][t-walsh] uses 1♦ for hearts,
1♥ for spades, and 1♠ for hands without a 4-card major.  Now opener can declare
responder's major, with an extra step to describe support.

[Walsh]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walsh_convention
[t-walsh]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_Walsh
[ric]: https://bridgewinners.com/article/view/is-fantunes-still-a-good-bidding-system/

| 1♣ - | |
|------|-|
| 1♦!  | 0+, 4+♥
| 1♥!  | 0+, 4+♠
| 1♠!  | 4+, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 1NT  | 8--11, 1--3♠, 1--3♥, no SPL
| 2♣!  | FG, 5+♦, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 2♦!  | 4--7, 6+♠♥
| 2♥!  | 8--11, 4+♥, 5+♠
| 2♠!  | FG, 5+♣, 0--3♠, 0--3♥, 0--4♦
| 2NT! | NF INV, 6+♦
| 3♣   | NF INV, 6+♣

The 2♥ response is [Reverse Flannery][rf] as in *Strawberry Polish Club* but can
be invitational (8--11) rather than merely constructive (6--9).
