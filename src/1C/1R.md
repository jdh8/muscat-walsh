# Red transfers 1♦♥

Opener completes the transfer with any balanced minimum, even with a doubleton,
and with 3-card support up to 17.  Completion is a free support double:
bypassing it denies 3-card support, except with 18--20 or with 15--17 and long
clubs.  Those hands bid 2NT!.  Balanced 18--20 hands rebid 1NT, as in [the
notrump ladder](../Openings.md#the-notrump-ladder).  Molenaar--Verbeek play
the same ladder.

Raises promise 4-card support: 2M with 11--14 and 3M with 15--17.  With
18--20, opener bids 3♦! without shortness, or splinters with a jump past 3M.

## Strong raises

Over 2NT!, a weak responder bids 3♣ to pass or correct.  Opener passes with
15--17 and long clubs, and otherwise describes the 18--20 hand naturally.
Responder's other bids are natural and forcing.

## After the completion

The completion is non-forcing.  Responder passes with 0--7, as the 1-level is
low enough to survive a 4--2 fit.  Other rebids are Checkback Gladiator, an XYZ
variant.  2♣ is a one-round force: a constructive signoff, an invitation, or a
special game force.  2♦ is an artificial game-forcing checkback.  Natural 2x
are invitations, and 3x are slam tries.

## After 1NT

Checkback Gladiator still applies.  This is Strawberry Polish Club's
1♣ - 1M - 1NT with about 6 points moved from responder to opener: responder
passes with 0--4, invites with 5--6, and forces to game with 7+.  Over 2♣,
opener shows support rather than a maximum, since 1NT is a single range.
