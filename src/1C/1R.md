# Red transfers 1♦♥

Opener completes the transfer with any balanced minimum, even with a doubleton,
and with 3-card support up to 17.  Completion is a free support double:
bypassing it denies 3-card support, except with 18--20.  Unbalanced ones bid
2NT.  Balanced ones rebid 1NT, as in [the notrump ladder][ladder].
[Molenaar--Verbeek][mv] play the same ladder.  After the completion or 1NT,
responder rebids by [Checkback Gladiator](1R/CheckbackGladiator.md).

Responder may hold nothing, so 3NT promises solid clubs.  Other 18--20 hands
with 6+♣ fake a 2♦ reverse unless they suit 1NT.

[ladder]: ../Openings.md#the-notrump-ladder
[mv]: https://www.bridgebond.nl/download.php?file=Wedstrijdzaken%2FSysteemkaarten%2FMK2526%2FNETH_OPEN_Molenaar_Verbeek+version+without+convention+names.pdf

Raises promise 4-card support: 2M with 11--14 and 3M with 15--17.  With
18--20, opener bids 4♣ with 6+ clubs, 3♦ without shortness, or otherwise
splinters with a jump past 3M.

## Strong raises

Over 2NT, a weak responder bids 3♣ to pass or correct.  Opener passes with
long clubs and otherwise describes the hand naturally.
Responder's other bids are natural and forcing.

| 1♣ - 1R -<br>2NT - | M = R + 1 |
|-----|---|
| 3♣! | P/C
| 3M  | S/O, 5+M
| 3NT | S/O
| 4M  | S/O

| 1♣ - 1R -<br>3♦ - | M = R + 1 |
|-----|---|
| 3M  | S/O
| 3NT | COG
| 4M  | S/O

Over 3♦, responder's other bids are cuebids, including 3♥ after 1♣ - 1♥.

## After a reverse

Responder plays [Blackout](1S.md#after-a-reverse) as after 1♠, but LEB is
0--7 since a transfer promises nothing.  LEB is the next step even when it is
responder's major, as opener holds at most two cards there.  Other bids are
natural and game-forcing.

| 1♣ - 1R -<br>2♦ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♥! | LEB, 0--7
| 3NT | S/O

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>2♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♠! | LEB, 0--7
| 3NT | S/O

Over LEB, opener with 18--20 bids the cheapest step, forcing one round.
Responder bids the next step with 0--3, and opener can then stop in three of a
minor or the reverse suit.  Other answers force to game.  After the 2♥
reverse, 2NT is that step, so 3♣ is any 16--17 hand without 5-5.

| 1♣ - 1R -<br>2♦ - 2♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♠! | 18--20, F1
| 2NT | 16--17
| 3♣  | 16--17, 6+♣
| 3♦  | 16--17, 5+♦, 5+♣

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>2♥ - 2♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 2NT! | 18--20, F1
| 3♣   | 16--17, no 5-5
| 3♥   | 16--17, 5+♥, 5+♣
