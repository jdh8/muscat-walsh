# Red transfers 1♦♥

Opener completes the transfer with any balanced minimum, even with a doubleton,
and with 3-card support up to 17.  Completion is a free support double:
bypassing it denies 3-card support, except with 18--20.  Unbalanced ones bid
2♦, an artificial strong raise.  Balanced ones rebid 1NT, as in
[the notrump ladder][ladder].  [Molenaar--Verbeek][mv] play the same ladder.
After the completion or 1NT, responder rebids by
[Checkback Gladiator](1R/CheckbackGladiator.md).

Responder may hold nothing, so 3NT promises solid clubs.  Other 18--20 hands
with 5+♣ bid 2NT with 0--2 cards in responder's major, or 2♦ with 3 cards,
unless they suit 1NT.

[ladder]: ../Openings.md#the-notrump-ladder
[mv]: https://www.bridgebond.nl/download.php?file=Wedstrijdzaken%2FSysteemkaarten%2FMK2526%2FNETH_OPEN_Molenaar_Verbeek+version+without+convention+names.pdf

18--20, opener bids 2♦ rather than reverse, so reverses deny 3-card support.

## After 2♦

Over the strong raise, the cheapest step 2♥ is a weak signoff with 0--5.  This
is [Odwrotka][odw] with the roles swapped.  Opener made the fit reverse, so
responder describes.  The 6+M rows show 8+ because 0--7 hands went through
[Multi 2♦](../1C.md#multi-2).

[odw]: https://polish.club/1C/1M/2D.html

| 1♣ - 1R -<br>2♦ - | M = R + 1 |
|------------|---|
| 2♥!        | 0--5
| 2♠!        | 6+, 4=M, no 5+ side suit
| 2NT!       | 6+, UNBAL 5=M
| 3♣♦        | 6+, 5+#, 4=M
| 3M         | 6+, BAL 5=M
| 3NT!       | 8+, 6+M, no SPL
| 3OM!, 4♣♦! | 8+, SPL, 0--1#, 6+M

## After a reverse

Responder plays [Blackout](1S.md#after-a-reverse) as after 1♠, but LEB is
0--7 since a transfer promises nothing.  LEB is the next step even though it is
responder's major, as opener holds at most two spades.  Other bids are natural
and game-forcing.

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>2♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♠! | LEB, 0--7
| 3NT | S/O
