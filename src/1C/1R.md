# Red transfers 1♦♥

Opener completes the transfer with exactly 3-card support and a minimum, and
raises to the 2-level with 4-card support and a minimum.  Either way responder
learns the fit at once, which is the support-double effect that natural 1M
responses only get after intervention.

Other rebids are natural, as if responder had bid the major.  Bypassing the
completion denies 3-card support with a minimum, so a minimum 2♣ has at most a
doubleton in the major.  A medium hand with 5+♣ and 3-card support bids 2♣
too, since the completion is limited to 14.

## After the completion

The completion is non-forcing.  Responder passes with a weak hand and 4--5
cards in the major, since the 7-card fit at the 1-level is the par contract.
Other rebids are as over a natural 1M response that opener raised to the
7-card fit: 1NT and 2♣ are weak, 2M is a weak 5-card suit, and 2♦ is an
artificial game-forcing checkback.
