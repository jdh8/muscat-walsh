# Red transfers 1♦♥

Opener completes the transfer with any balanced minimum, even with a doubleton,
and with 3-card support up to 17.  Completion is a free support double:
bypassing it denies 3-card support, except with 18--20, or with 15--17 and
6+♣.  Unbalanced 18--20 hands reverse or raise.  Balanced ones rebid 1NT, as
in [the notrump ladder][ladder].  [Molenaar--Verbeek][mv] play the same
ladder.  After the completion or 1NT, responder rebids by
[Checkback Gladiator](1R/CheckbackGladiator.md).

2NT is invitational or better, 15--20, with 3-card support and 6+♣.  Without
3-card support, 18--20 hands with 6+♣ bid 3NT unless they suit 1NT.

[ladder]: ../Openings.md#the-notrump-ladder
[mv]: https://www.bridgebond.nl/download.php?file=Wedstrijdzaken%2FSysteemkaarten%2FMK2526%2FNETH_OPEN_Molenaar_Verbeek+version+without+convention+names.pdf

Raises promise 4-card support: 2M with 11--14, 3M with 15--17, and 4M with
18--20.  With 18--20 and shortness, opener splinters in 3♦ or the other major
instead.

The 2♣ rebid stops at 15: a 16--17 hand with 5+♣ and no support either
reverses, jumps to 3♣, or is 5332 and opened 1NT.  Reverses stay 16--20
because they are forcing.

## After 2NT

Over 2NT, a weak responder signs off in 3♣, or in 3M with a 5-card major.
3♦ is a relay with 6+: opener bids 3M with 15--17, and responder passes with
6--8.  Other rebids show 18--20 and force to game.

| 1♣ - 1R -<br>2NT - | M = R + 1 |
|-----|---|
| 3♣  | S/O, 0--5
| 3♦! | (R), 6+
| 3M  | S/O, 0--5, 5=M
| 3NT | S/O
| 4M  | S/O

## After a reverse

Responder plays [Blackout](1S.md#after-a-reverse) as after 1♠, but LEB is
0--7 since a transfer promises nothing.  LEB is the next step even when it is
responder's major, as opener holds 3-card support only with 18--20.  Other
bids are natural and game-forcing.

| 1♣ - 1R -<br>2♦ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♥! | LEB, 0--7
| 3NT | S/O

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>2♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♠! | LEB, 0--7
| 3NT | S/O
