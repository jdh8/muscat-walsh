# Transfer to 1NT

The 1♠ response denies a 4-card major and invitational values.  Opener
completes the transfer with any minimum, including a minimum with a 4-card
major, because responder cannot hold a fit.  Opener bids naturally with
extras or with a second suit worth showing below 1NT.

Diamonds are a natural non-forcing rebid here.  Responder either holds
diamonds or is balanced, so there is no need to reverse with 4♦ and 5+♣.

| 1♣ - 1♠! - | |
|-----|-|
| 1NT | 11--14
| 2♣  | 11--17, UNBAL, 5+♣
| 2♦  | 11--17, 4+♦, 5+♣
| 2M  | REV, 16+, 4+M, 5+♣
| 2NT | BAL 18--19
| 3♣  | 15--17, 6+♣
| 3♦  | FG, 4+♦, 5+♣

Responder's rebids over 1NT are to play, except the game-forcing jump to 3♦.
There is no invitation, as 1♣ - 1NT already covers balanced invitations.  An
invitational hand with long diamonds signs off in 2♦ or gambles on 3NT.

| 1♣ - 1♠! -<br>1NT - | |
|----|-|
| 2♣ | NF, 4+♣
| 2♦ | NF, 5+♦
| 3♦ | FG, 5+♦
