# No-major 1♠

1♣ - 1♠! denies a 4-card major.  Responder holds one of:

- 4--7 without a major
- 8--11 with a splinter in a major, unless a 6-card minor jumps directly
- 12+ balanced: with no 4-card major and no 5-card minor, that is 33(43) or (32)44

A singleton ace or king is not a splinter.  8--11 hands with one bid 1NT.

Opener never shows a major with a balanced hand.  Responder has at most three,
so a 4-3 fit is not worth the search.  A 4414 hand bids notrump as if
balanced, since responder is likely to hold diamonds.  Other rebids follow [red
transfers](1R.md).

| 1♣ - 1♠ - | |
|-----|-|
| 1NT | (BAL 12--14) or (11--17, 4414)
| 2♣  | UNBAL 11--17, 5+♣
| 2♦  | REV, 16--20, 4+♦, 5+♣
| 2♥♠ | REV, 16--20, 4+#, 5+♣
| 2NT | (BAL 18--20) or (18--20, 4414)
| 3♣  | 15--17, 6+♣
| 3NT | 18--20, 6+♣

## After 1NT

Responder signs off in a minor or 3NT, or splinters to invite.  Opener rejects
with 2NT or 3♣, and accepts with 3♦ or 3NT.  Notrump promises a stop in the
splinter suit.  Over 3♣, responder passes or corrects to 3♦.

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - | |
|------|-|
| 2♣♦  | S/O
| 2♥♠! | INV, SPL, 0--1#
| 3NT  | S/O
| 4NT  | INV, 19--20

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - 2♥♠! - | |
|-----|-|
| 2NT | NF, MIN, stop
| 3♣  | NF, MIN, no stop
| 3♦  | FG, MAX, no stop
| 3NT | MAX, stop

## After 2NT

Responder passes with 4--5, and raises with 6+ or a good 5.

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>2NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♣♦ | F, 5+#
| 3NT | S/O
| 4NT | INV, 12--13
| 6NT | S/O

## After 2♣

Responder passes with a weak hand.  Opener answers the splinter as over 1NT.

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>2♣ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♦   | S/O, 5+♦
| 2♥♠! | INV, SPL, 0--1#
| 3♣   | FG, 4+♣
| 3NT  | S/O

## After a reverse

Responder plays the [Blackout convention][Blackout]: the next step is LEB, a
weak relay with 4--7.  Over 2♠, that step is 2NT.  Other bids are natural and
game-forcing.

[Blackout]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackout_convention

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>2♦ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♥! | LEB, 4--7
| 2NT | FG
| 3♣♦ | FG
| 3NT | S/O

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>2♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♠! | LEB, 4--7
| 2NT | FG
| 3♣♦ | FG
| 3♥  | FG, 3=♥
| 3NT | S/O

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>2♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 2NT! | LEB, 4--7
| 3♣♦  | FG
| 3♠   | FG, 3=♠
| 3NT  | S/O

Over LEB, opener with 16--17 bids 2NT if available, 3♣ with six clubs, or
repeats the reverse suit with 5-5.  Over 2♠ - 2NT!, 3♣ is any 16--17 hand
without 5-5.  Anything else shows 18--20 and forces to game.  After a 16--17
answer, responder passes or signs off in 3♣♦.
