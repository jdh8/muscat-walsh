# Game-forcing minors 2♣♠

Responder bids 2♣ with 5+♦ or 2♠ with 5+♣, game-forcing without a 4-card
major.  With both minors, responder bids 2♣ and shows clubs next.

Opener describes, and responder places the contract.  Opener's rebid shows
shape, not strength, except that balanced hands split 12--14 from 18--20.

Both hands show stops the same way: a new major shows a stop, and notrump shows
stops in all unbid suits.  Over a splinter, 3NT promises a stop in the short
suit.  Bids beyond the tables are slam tries.

## After 2♣

Opener never holds a 5-card major, so a major shows four cards and a stop.
Opener bids a 4-card major first, hearts with both, unless raising diamonds.

## After 2♠

Opener bids 3NT with the weak balanced hand and 2NT with the strong one,
keeping room where slam is possible.

| 3♦♥♠! | SPL, 0--1#
