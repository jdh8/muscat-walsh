# Competitive bidding

Over a (1♦) or (1♥) overcall, double is the stolen Transfer Walsh bid.  This
is an upgrade of the negative double: opener completes the transfer at the
1-level with a minimum and 3-card support, or raises with 4-card support, so
the support double is built in.  A plain negative double cannot tell 3-5 from
4-4 fits in both majors over (1♦); the transfers can.

The 1♠ response keeps transferring to 1NT.  It keeps the bidding open and
partly asks for a stopper, so opener's 1NT promises one.

The same scheme applies to the 1♦ opening over (1♥).

| 1♣ (1♦) | |
|-----|-|
| X!  | TRF, 6+, 4+♥
| 1♥! | TRF, 6+, 4+♠
| 1♠! | TRF to 1NT, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 2♦! | TRF, 6+♥
| 2♥! | TRF, 6+♠

| 1♣ (1♥) | |
|-----|-|
| X!  | TRF, 6+, 4+♠
| 1♠! | TRF to 1NT, 0--3♠
| 2♥! | TRF, 6+♠

The 2-level transfers carry both weak hands and strong invitations or better,
like the direct jump shift and the game-forcing suit at once.  The opener
completes the transfer with a minimum, and responder passes when weak or
continues when strong.

| 1♣ (1♦) X - | |
|-----|-|
| 1♥! | 11--14, 3=♥
| 1♠  | 11--17, 4+♠, 0--2♥
| 1NT | BAL 12--14, ♦ stopper, 0--2♥
| 2♥  | 11--14, 4+♥
| 3♥  | 15--17, 4+♥
