# Contested 1♣

The free bids follow the uncontested transfers:

- 1♦ or (1♦) X: transfer, 4+♥
- 1♥ or (1♥) X: transfer, 4+♠
- 1♠: transfer to 1NT, partly ask for stopper
- 2♣: transfer, 5+♦
- 2♠: transfer, 5+♣

Opener's rebids change because the free bids are constructive:

- 1NT is a balanced minimum, 12--14, with a stopper.  The balanced 18--20
  bids 2NT, also with a stopper.
- The cuebid 2♦ is an artificial 18--20 raise rather than a reverse.
- 3NT still promises solid clubs.  Other 18--20 hands with 6+♣ bid 2NT with a
  stopper, or cuebid without.

## Over (X)

Natural notrump is not very useful after a double, so let's also use it for
transfers.  Then 1♠ takes care of diamonds, the only suit left over.  With
0--5, responder passes or preempts.

|------|-|
| XX   | 10+, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 1♦!  | TRF, 6+, 4+♥
| 1♥!  | TRF, 6+, 4+♠
| 1♠!  | TRF, 6+, 5+♦
| 1NT! | TRF, 6+, 5+♣
| 2♣   | PRE, 0--7, 5+♣
| 2x   | PRE, 0--7, 6+#
| 2NT! | TRF: (PRE, 7+♣) or (FG, 6+♣)

| 3♦!  | TRF: (PRE, 7+♥) or (FG, 6+♥)
| 3♥!  | TRF: (PRE, 7+♠) or (FG, 6+♠)

## Over (2♣+)

Double is negative.  New suits are natural and forcing, and 2NT is natural
and invitational with a stopper.

## Opener's rebids

### After XX

The key feature of XX is to start penalty process.  Our further doubles are
penalty rather than takeout.

| 1♣ (X) XX - | |
|------|-|
| P    | 5+♣
| 1♦♥♠ | NAT, 4+#
| 1NT  | BAL 12--14, 2--3♣

| 2NT  | BAL 18--20
| 3♣   | 15--17, 6+♣

### After a transfer to 1M

One step below 1M, including the stolen X, is a transfer to 1M.  Bid as if
[1♣ - 1R](../1C/1R.md) most of the time, with the changes in the [chapter](../Competition.md) and above.  Over (1♥),
opener gets two cuebids: 2♦ stays the raise, and 2♥ takes the 18--20 hands
with neither support nor a stopper.  Over (1♦), the 2♦ raise takes those
too.

Over the 2♦ raise, responder describes as in [Odwrotka][odw] with the roles
swapped: opener made the fit reverse, so responder describes.  The free bid
promised 6+, so there is no weak signoff.  The 6+M rows show 8+, since weaker
hands bid the suit directly.

[odw]: https://polish.club/1C/1M/2D.html

| 1♣ (1x) TRF -<br>2♦ - | M = TRF + 1 |
|------------|---|
| 2♠!        | 6+, 4=M, no 5+ side suit
| 2NT!       | 6+, UNBAL 5=M
| 3♣♦        | 6+, 5+#, 4=M
| 3M         | 6+, BAL 5=M
| 3NT!       | 8+, 6+M, no SPL
| 3OM!, 4♣♦! | 8+, SPL, 0--1#, 6+M

After the completion or 1NT, responder rebids by
[Checkback Gladiator](../1C/1R/CheckbackGladiator.md) as after the uncontested
completion, since opener is minimum either way.  The cuebid 2♦ is still the
game-forcing checkback.

### After the negative double

This is the only 1-level X response not a stolen transfer.  It is more urgent
to find 4-4 heart fits than to transfer to 1NT.  There is no 1-level
completion, so the 2♥ raise takes the 3-card support.

| 1♣ (1♠) X - | |
|-----|-|
| 1NT | BAL 12--14, 0--2♥, stop
| 2♣  | 11--17, 5+♣, 0--2♥
| 2♦! | 18--20, 3+♥
| 2♥  | (11--17, 3=♥) or (11--14, 4+♥)
| 2♠! | 18--20, 0--2♥, no stop
| 2NT | 18--20, 0--2♥, stop
| 3♣  | 15--17, 6+♣, 0--2♥
| 3♥  | 15--17, 4+♥
| 3NT | 18--20, SOL 6+♣, 0--2♥

### After a transfer to 1NT

Opener bids 1NT with a stopper and 12--14, or otherwise rebids as over the
uncontested [1♠](../1C/1S.md).  The cuebid replaces 2NT with 18--20 but no stopper.
