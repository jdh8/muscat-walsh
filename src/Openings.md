## Choice between minor suits

- By default, open the longer minor, or 1♦ with equal length.
- Open 1♦ with 11--14, 4+♦, 5=♣.
- Open 1♣ with 18--20, balanced 4=♦.

## The notrump ladder

- **12--14:** 1♣ - 1♦♥ - 1♥♠
- **15--17:** 1NT
- **18--20:** 1♣ - 1♦♥ - 1NT
