# Openings

|      | |
|------|-|
| 1♣!  | (11--23, 2+♣) or (21--23, 4441)
| 1♦   | 11--20, 4+♦
| 1♥♠  | 11--20, 5+#
| 1NT  | 15--17, 2--5♠, 2--5♥, 2--6♦, 2--6♣
| 2♣!  | ART STR, 21+

Prefer 1♦ to 1♣ when holding (xx)45.  A 1♣ opener has no cheap way to show
4+♦ after a transfer response, because 2♦ is a reverse.  This canapé deviates
from standard natural bidding.

## The notrump ladder

- **12--14:** 1♣ - 1♦♥ - 1♥♠
- **15--17:** 1NT
- **18--20:** 1♣ - 1♦♥ - 1NT
- **21--23:** 1♣ - 1♦♥ - 2NT
- **24+:** 2♣
